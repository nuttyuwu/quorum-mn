Who we are

We’re Quorum, a software studio in Mongolia. We make tools and write practical guides in Mongolian and English.

Our mission

We start by watching how people do a job. Then we write and test software for the steps that repeat, so the person doing the work can check the result.

Our goal

We want small teams to have time to welcome a guest, help a customer or simply finish work on time.
